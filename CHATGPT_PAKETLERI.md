# Makarya · ChatGPT paketleri (kopyala-yapıştır)

**Nerede:** Makarya görsellerini ürettiğin mevcut ChatGPT sohbetinde.
**Nasıl:** Aşağıdaki 3 mesajı sırayla, her birini ayrı mesaj olarak yapıştır.
**ChatGPT durursa:** Sadece `devam` yaz.
**Bitince:** Görselleri indir ve bana gönder. Her görselin altında ChatGPT'nin yazdığı dosya adı olacak. Adlandırmayı, küçültmeyi ve oyuna eklemeyi ben yaparım.

Toplam: 15 görsel. Hepsi erkek karakterle. Kız karakter görselleri, erkek hikâyesi bitince bitmiş erkek görsellerinden türetilecek.

---

## Mesaj 0 · Kurallar (bir kez)

```
We are continuing the Makarya game illustrations from this chat. I need 15 new images in two packages. Rules for EVERY image from now on:

1. Exactly the same art style, lighting and world as the Makarya images you already made in this chat (painterly animated-film concept art, warm golden-hour light, cozy Istanbul details).
2. The main character is the same curly-haired boy as before, now older as each prompt says. Keep his face consistent with the earlier images.
3. Landscape 3:2 (about 1536x1024) unless I say portrait.
4. His face must be clearly visible in the upper two-thirds; the bottom 25% will be covered by a caption.
5. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, readable signs or speech bubbles in the image.
6. The game's tone is humorous, sometimes darkly comic: show the funny tension of the moment in expressions and small background details.
7. Under each image write ONLY its file name, e.g. "sahne/ilk-ders.png", so I can match them.
8. Generate the images in the package one after another in the same reply. If you have to stop, stop cleanly; when I write "devam", continue from the next image in the package.

Reply "Hazırım" and wait for Package 1.
```
