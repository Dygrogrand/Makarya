# Makarya · ChatGPT görsel paketleri (kopyala-yapıştır)

**Nerede:** Makarya görsellerini ürettiğin mevcut ChatGPT sohbetinde.
**Nasıl:** Mesajları sırayla, her birini ayrı mesaj olarak yapıştır.
**ChatGPT durursa:** `devam` yaz.
**ChatGPT eski bir görseli bulamazsa:** Yenilenecek görsellerin orijinalleri ayrıca gönderdiğim `eski-gorseller.zip` paketinde. İlgili dosyayı mesaja ekle.
**Bitince:** Görselleri indir ve paket paket bana gönder. Adlandırmayı, küçültmeyi ve oyuna eklemeyi ben yaparım.

Toplam 71 görsel:
- 12 okul yılları sahnesi
- 14 kurallara uymayan eski görselin yenisi
- 1 yetişkin karakter kartı
- 28 mekân arka planı
- 16 büyük dönüm noktası sahnesi

## Mesaj 0 · Kurallar (bir kez; önceki kuralların yerine geçer)

```
We are continuing the Makarya game illustrations in this chat. From now on these rules replace all earlier rules and apply to EVERY image — new ones and remakes of old ones.

STYLE (keep exactly as before)
- Same painterly animated-film concept-art style, warm golden-hour light and cozy details as the Makarya images you already made.
- Landscape 3:2 (about 1536x1024) unless I say portrait. The main character's face in the upper two-thirds; the bottom 25% will be covered by a caption.
- The main character is the same curly-haired boy, aged as each prompt says. Keep his face consistent across all ages.
- Humorous, sometimes darkly comic tone: show the funny tension of the moment in expressions and small background details.

WORLD: Makarya is a FICTIONAL country that resembles Turkey, set in the present day. Its city is a fictional hilly seaside city with invented towers and generic domed rooftops. It must NOT be recognizable as any real city.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN in every image:
1. Any text, letters, numbers, words, signs with writing, labels, price tags, newspapers, book titles, mugs or T-shirts with words, speech bubbles, currency symbols.
2. Any real brand, logo or recognizable product design: no chip/candy/drink packaging you can identify, no chocolate eggs, no three-stripe or swoosh shoes, no phone brand camera designs. Everything must be generic and unbranded.
3. Any real landmark: no Galata Tower, no Bosphorus Bridge, no identifiable real mosque or palace, no real skyline, no real tram models.
4. Any national flag, political symbol or party color scheme.
5. Religious symbols or places of worship in the foreground (a distant generic dome on the skyline is fine).
6. Real people or celebrity look-alikes.

Under each image write ONLY its file name (e.g. "sahne/ilk-ders.png"). Generate the images of a package one after another in the same reply; if you have to stop, stop cleanly and continue from the next image when I write "devam".

Reply "Hazırım" and wait for Package 1.
```
