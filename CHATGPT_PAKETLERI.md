# Makarya · ChatGPT paketleri (kopyala-yapıştır)

**Nerede:** Makarya görsellerini ürettiğin mevcut ChatGPT sohbetinde.
**Nasıl:** Aşağıdaki mesajları sırayla, her birini ayrı mesaj olarak yapıştır. Her pakette en fazla 10 görsel var.
**ChatGPT durursa:** Sadece `devam` yaz.
**Bitince:** Görselleri istediğin adla indir ve paket paket bana gönder. Her görselin altında ChatGPT'nin yazdığı dosya adı olacak. Adlandırmayı, küçültmeyi ve oyuna eklemeyi ben yaparım.

Toplam: 8 mesaj, 68 görsel.

---

## Mesaj 0 · Kurallar (bir kez)

```
We are continuing the Makarya game illustrations from this chat. I need 68 new images, delivered in packages of up to 10. Rules for EVERY image from now on:

1. Exactly the same art style, lighting and world as the Makarya images you already made in this chat (painterly animated-film concept art, warm golden-hour light, cozy Istanbul details).
2. Landscape 3:2 (about 1536x1024) unless I say portrait.
3. The main child's face must be clearly visible in the upper two-thirds; the bottom 25% will be covered by a caption.
4. ABSOLUTELY NO text, letters, numbers, logos, readable signs or speech bubbles in the image.
5. There is a new GIRL protagonist. After you create her character sheet, she must look the same in every image (same face, dark curly hair in a small half-ponytail with a red hair clip, olive skin, big dark brown eyes), aged as each prompt says.
6. Under each image write ONLY its file name, e.g. "kiz/sahne/dogum.png", so I can match them.
7. Generate the images in the package one after another in the same reply. If you have to stop, stop cleanly; when I write "devam", continue from the next image in the package.

Reply "Hazırım" and wait for Package 1.
```
