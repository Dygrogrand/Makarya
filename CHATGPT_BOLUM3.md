## Kurulum mesajı
CHAPTER III PACKAGE (ages 6 to 9, primary school years). 15 images, ONE image per reply; after each image wait for my next message.
Rules for every image in this chapter:
- Style: exactly the same painterly storybook illustration style as your last 12 images (soft gouache-like textures, warm light). Fully original character designs; nothing that resembles a known film, cartoon or franchise.
- EVERY IMAGE IS A STANDALONE SCENE with its own fresh cast. Do not carry side characters between images, except: the protagonist, his family (mother, father, grandmother, grandfather, the family cat) and, ONLY in classroom/school scenes, the class teacher.
- Protagonist: the boy from the character sheet (dark brown WAVY hair, ONE tuft standing up at the crown, warm brown eyes, thick dark eyebrows, light olive skin) at the age given; he appears EXACTLY ONCE per image. Nobody else has dark wavy hair with a crown tuft.
- Mother: long straight dark-brown hair in a low ponytail, freckles, slim. Father: short neat dark hair, full trimmed beard, heavier build. Grandmother: silver bun, round glasses, patterned cardigan. Family cat: black-and-white.
- Class teacher (school scenes only): tall thin woman, short grey bob, long cardigan, reading glasses on a chain.
- School look: kids wear blue school smocks with white collars.
- NO text anywhere (no letters, numbers, signs, logos, readable screens, notebooks or report cards — any writing is unreadable squiggles). NO domes, minarets, flags or real-city skylines. No injuries.
- Landscape 3:2; the boy's face clearly visible in the upper two-thirds; bottom quarter visually quiet (a caption covers it).
